# Peer Group Curation

Research date: 2026-04-27

Purpose: define reviewed peer groups for descriptive benchmark context. These groups are not investment recommendations and do not imply that any stock is cheap, expensive, or fairly valued.

## Curation Rules

- Prefer Oslo-listed peers where business fit is reasonable.
- Then prefer Nordic listed peers.
- Then use European listed peers.
- Use international peers when the Nordic/European universe is too sparse.
- Mark groups as `reviewed`, not `trusted`, until the user has accepted the peer logic after using it.
- Keep peer notes explicit where business model, geography, scale, or segment mix differs.
- Free fundamentals remain screening-grade only; sector KPIs are still needed for stronger context.

## Reviewed Groups

### NOD.OL: Low-Power Wireless Semiconductors

Focus: Nordic Semiconductor is a low-power wireless connectivity and IoT semiconductor company.

Reviewed peers:

- `UBXN.SW`: Swiss IoT positioning/wireless module peer; closer end-market overlap, different module/system mix.
- `SLAB`: close wireless MCU and IoT connectivity peer.
- `STM`: European broad semiconductor peer with microcontrollers, wireless, sensors, automotive, and industrial exposure.
- `IFX.DE`: European semiconductor peer with IoT, power, automotive, and security exposure.
- `NXPI`: embedded processing/connectivity peer with automotive and industrial exposure.
- `ON`: mixed-signal and power semiconductor peer; less direct wireless overlap.

Key caveat: there is no close Oslo-listed semiconductor peer.

### MOWI.OL: Atlantic Salmon Farming

Focus: listed Atlantic salmon farming and vertically integrated seafood.

Reviewed peers:

- `SALM.OL`: large Norwegian salmon farmer.
- `LSG.OL`: Norwegian seafood group with salmon plus whitefish/wild-catch exposure.
- `GSF.OL`: Norwegian salmon farmer.
- `BAKKA.OL`: Faroese/Scottish salmon farmer listed in Oslo.
- `AUSS.OL`: seafood holding company with Leroy ownership and pelagic exposure.
- `MAS.OL`: smaller Norwegian salmon farmer.
- `SALME.OL`: adjacent land-based salmon exposure; different model and risk profile.

Key caveat: stronger context needs harvest volume, EBIT/kg, licence geography, biological cost, feed exposure, and contract/spot exposure.

### FRO.OL: Crude Tankers

Focus: crude tanker owners/operators with VLCC, Suezmax, and adjacent Aframax/LR2 exposure.

Reviewed peers:

- `OET.OL`: Oslo/NYSE listed VLCC and Suezmax crude tanker peer.
- `DHT`: VLCC-focused crude tanker peer.
- `TNK`: Suezmax/Aframax crude tanker peer.
- `INSW`: mixed crude/product tanker peer.
- `NAT`: Suezmax-focused crude tanker peer.

Key caveat: tanker context should use NAV, fleet age, vessel class mix, charter coverage, scrubber exposure, and spot-rate exposure before drawing any conclusion.

### HAFNI.OL: Product Tankers

Focus: product and chemical tanker owners/operators.

Reviewed peers:

- `TORM.CO`: Danish listed product tanker peer.
- `STNG`: large listed product tanker peer.
- `ASC`: product and chemical tanker peer.
- `DIS.MI`: European product tanker peer.
- `INSW`: mixed crude/product tanker peer.

Key caveat: Hafnia should not be merged into a crude-tanker-only peer set. Product tanker comparisons need clean/dirty product exposure, chemical capability, and pool/spot exposure.

### DOFG.OL: Subsea And Offshore Services

Focus: subsea services, offshore support vessels, IMR/light construction, and adjacent offshore services.

Reviewed peers:

- `SUBC.OL`: subsea engineering/construction and offshore project services.
- `SOFF.OL`: offshore support vessel owner/operator.
- `AKSO.OL`: offshore engineering/products/services, adjacent rather than direct.
- `TDW`: international OSV peer.
- `FTI`: global subsea systems and services peer.
- `OII`: ROV/subsea services and offshore engineered services peer.
- `HLX`: offshore energy services and well intervention peer.

Key caveat: DOF should not be benchmarked directly against drilling-rig owners.

### ODL.OL: Offshore Drilling Rigs

Focus: offshore drilling contractors, especially harsh-environment floaters.

Reviewed peers:

- `NOL.OL`: harsh-environment semi-submersible exposure.
- `SDRL.OL`: global deepwater drilling contractor.
- `BORR.OL`: Oslo-listed jack-up drilling contractor; useful Oslo context, not direct floater peer.
- `SHLF.OL`: Oslo-listed jack-up drilling contractor; useful Oslo context, not direct floater peer.
- `RIG`: global offshore drilling contractor with ultra-deepwater and harsh-environment exposure.
- `VAL`: global offshore driller with floaters and jack-ups.
- `NE`: global offshore drilling contractor.

Key caveat: rig type, harsh-environment capability, contract backlog, day rates, utilisation, leverage, and ownership/management model matter.

### KOG.OL: Defence, Aerospace And Maritime Technology

Focus: European defence/aerospace primes, defence electronics, sensors, missiles, maritime technology, and relevant Nordic defence specialists.

Reviewed peers:

- `KIT.OL`: Oslo industrial/electronics supplier proxy, not a defence prime.
- `SAAB-B.ST`: closest Nordic defence/aerospace prime.
- `MILDEF.ST`: Nordic defence IT and rugged hardware specialist.
- `IVSO.ST`: Nordic tactical communication/hearing protection specialist.
- `RHM.DE`: European defence systems peer.
- `HAG.DE`: European defence sensor/electronics specialist.
- `HO.PA`: European defence, aerospace, cyber, digital identity, and secure communications peer.
- `LDO.MI`: European aerospace, defence, electronics, cyber, and space peer.
- `BA.L`: large UK defence/aerospace prime.
- `AIR.PA`: Airbus Defence and Space exposure, but group is dominated by commercial aerospace.

Key caveat: Kongsberg's proposed maritime demerger means historical peer context may need to split between defence/discovery and maritime.

### LINK.OL: CPaaS And Customer Messaging

Focus: CPaaS, mobile messaging, and adjacent customer engagement software.

Reviewed peers:

- `SINCH.ST`: closest listed Nordic CPaaS/cloud communications peer.
- `CMCOM.AS`: European CPaaS and conversational commerce/customer engagement peer.
- `TWLO`: global cloud communications/API platform peer.
- `BRZE`: customer engagement platform with messaging channels; adjacent software peer.
- `KVYO`: marketing automation and messaging/email platform; adjacent software peer.
- `ROUTE.NS`: CPaaS/mobile messaging peer with different geography and ownership context.

Key caveat: LINK has no close Oslo-listed peer. Gross margin, organic growth, message volumes, regional mix, operator pass-through costs, and leverage are important.
